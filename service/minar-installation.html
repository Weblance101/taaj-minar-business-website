RCC Mosque Minar Installation

Installing a pre-cast RCC minar requires careful handling, suitable lifting equipment and accurate positioning. TAJ MINAR provides minar installation support for mosque construction and renovation projects, coordinating transport, rigging, crane hoisting, alignment and base anchoring according to project requirements.

Installation work can include precision plumb alignment, structural anchoring and mounting of decorative elements such as a steel Chand. Our approach is focused on proper placement, secure fixing and a clean architectural finish for mosque minars.
