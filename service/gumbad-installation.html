Gumbad (Mosque Dome) Installation

Pre-cast RCC and modular mosque dome installation with careful placement, waterproofing and finishing solutions.

RCC Gumbad Dome Installation

A Gumbad is an important architectural feature of a mosque, combining traditional Islamic design with practical structural requirements. TAJ MINAR provides pre-cast RCC and modular Gumbad solutions in onion-shaped, hemispherical and pointed designs, with dome sizes described in the project specifications.

Our installation service can include careful crane hoisting and placement, alignment, joint treatment, waterproofing and steel crescent moon mounting. We focus on a neat installation process and durable finishing suitable for mosque construction and renovation projects.
