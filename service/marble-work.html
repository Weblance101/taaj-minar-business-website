Mosque Marble & Stone Craftsmanship

Marble adds a distinctive finish to mosque interiors and architectural details. TAJ MINAR & TAJ SALES provides marble solutions for Mihrab arches, Qibla walls, Islamic calligraphy panels, Jali screens and mosque flooring, with designs selected to suit the project's architectural requirements.

Our marble work can include Makrana white marble elements, carved and patterned surfaces, calligraphy inlay, lattice detailing and polished floor layouts. These solutions are suitable for new mosque construction, renovation and interior architectural finishing projects.
