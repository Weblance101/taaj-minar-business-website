About TAJ MINAR

Mosque Construction & Islamic Architectural Solutions

TAJ MINAR provides complete mosque construction and Islamic architectural solutions across India, from new mosque projects and renovations to minar manufacturing, Gumbad installation, marble Mihrab work, flooring, carpets, and essential mosque products.

We combine traditional Mughal and Indo-Islamic architectural aesthetics with practical civil and structural solutions. Whether you are planning a new mosque, renovating an existing prayer space, installing RCC minars or Gumbads, creating a marble Mihrab, upgrading mosque flooring, or furnishing prayer halls with carpets and mats, we offer coordinated solutions for the project.
