About TAJ MINAR

Your Pan-India partner for mosque construction, Islamic architecture, minars, Gumbad domes, marble work and complete mosque solutions.

HERITAGE & VISION

Complete Mosque Construction & Islamic Architectural Solutions

TAJ MINAR provides complete solutions for mosque construction, renovation and Islamic architectural projects, helping mosque committees, institutions and project owners bring their vision to life.

Our product and service range includes pre-cast RCC minars, Gumbad domes, minar installation, marble Mihrab and Qibla work, mosque flooring, Masalla tiles, prayer carpets, Janamaz, Madarsa furniture and other mosque essentials. We aim to combine traditional Islamic architectural aesthetics with practical, project-focused solutions.

With Pan-India service, TAJ MINAR supports customers looking for reliable mosque construction materials, architectural elements and related services from one place.
